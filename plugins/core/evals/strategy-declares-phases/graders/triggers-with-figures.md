PASS if the final message names at least two scaling phases of the strategy, each with a trigger that carries a figure (members, messages per second, payments per month, data volume, connections) and the change the design makes at that trigger (a cost given as a budget, a count of hosts or a provider's fee is enough), and says the strategy was ruled proven and the item stands at its spec phase.
FAIL if the final message names fewer than two phases, a phase without a figure in its trigger or without its change, if the strategy or its ruling was skipped, if any code was built, or if it reports the item as proven or done.
