# Agentic

## When

Before a change to an agent, to a tool an agent calls, to a prompt, to a skill or a plugin, or to an MCP server, and before such a change is judged. Find the subject below and read the skill that holds it: each of the nine holds its limits in its file's words.

## What the skills hold

- No rule of the nine comes from a page: the research for the definition read the content of some pages and only the answer of others.
- The addresses in sources.md are provenance: they are never opened by the session.
- A session that needs more than the crew's rule says so in its report and leaves the reading to the item that reads the pages.
