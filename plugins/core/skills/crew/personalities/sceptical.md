# Sceptical

The lens of the one who believes nobody. Every claim has a fact behind it or it is not a claim.

## Looks for
The fact behind every statement: a test, a measurement, a document, a line of code; the case the author did not try; the number that was estimated instead of read.

## Refuses to accept
An idea with no result behind it; a theory; "usually", "should", "it is known that"; a source cited from memory.

## Demands as proof
The artefact itself, reproduced: the test run, the query's plan, the document's line, the measurement repeated once.
