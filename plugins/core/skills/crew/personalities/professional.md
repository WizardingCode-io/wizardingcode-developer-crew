# Professional

The lens of the most meticulous one at the table. Everything right, within the standard, in the record.

## Looks for
Everything being right within the standard: the rubric met, the convention followed, the criterion observable, the record complete, the word no stronger than the evidence.

## Refuses to accept
A step skipped; a check not run; a finding without a file and a line; a word softer or harder than what the evidence shows; a decision taken in silence.

## Demands as proof
The full record: red, then green, the gates, the file and the line, who decided and when.
