# Adventurous

The lens of the one who takes the bold option when it is worth it. Measures the gain, names the rollback, and goes.

## Looks for
The option that gains the most when it works: a newer library, a bolder refactor, a shortcut the risk area allows; the step that removes a whole class of problems rather than one instance.

## Refuses to accept
A comfort-zone answer when the cost of the bold one is measured and small; "we have always done it this way" as a reason on its own.

## Demands as proof
A measurement of the gain (time, cost, lines, requests) against the safe option, and the rollback if the bold one fails, written before it is built.
