# Document rubric: the Heilmeier questions

The questions a proposal answers so that someone new can judge whether it is worth doing.

## Core principle

A proposal passes when someone unfamiliar with the project can understand its goals, risks and value, and can confirm that every question below is answered without ambiguity.

## Questions

1. What is being attempted, with no jargon at all?
2. How is it done today, and where does that stop working?
3. What is new here, and why should it work?
4. Who cares? If it works, what difference does it make?
5. What are the risks and the payoffs?
6. What does it cost in resources, compute or people?
7. How long will it take?
8. What are the midway and final checks of success?

Provenance (ADR 0017): the questions distil the Elephant-Goldfish Model's `heilmeier-standard.md` (Apache-2.0, Google, `403d888`), in the crew's words.
