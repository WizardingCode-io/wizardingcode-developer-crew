# Sources of the Strategy skill and the e-commerce playbook

One line per address the two skills cite: the address, its title, what it is, and the date the research for the frontend team's definition read the page itself. A source known only from a search summary is not here and no rule cites it. The research's notes stay on the machine that made them; this file is what the repository keeps of them.

## Usability benchmarks

- https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate · Baymard Institute, "50 Cart Abandonment Rate Statistics" · a survey of US shoppers and an aggregation of 50 studies; the page gives no sample size or year for the reasons survey · page dated 2026-10-28 · read on 2026-11-16
- https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-of-checkout-ux · Baymard Institute, "The Current State of Checkout UX" · a usability benchmark, 44,000+ checkout scores; published 2024-11-13, updated 2026-11-03 · read on 2026-11-16
- https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-navigation-best-practice · Baymard Institute, "Homepage and Category Navigation UX 2026" · a usability benchmark, 17,000+ scores over 180+ US and European sites; published 2024-08-06, updated 2026-10-13 · read on 2026-11-16
- https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-product-list-and-filtering · Baymard Institute, "Product List UX 2025" · a usability benchmark, 21,000+ scores over 170+ sites; published 2024-08-22, updated 2025-09-09 · read on 2026-11-16
- https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-search-query-types · Baymard Institute, "Ecommerce Search UX 2026" · a usability benchmark, 10,000+ scores over 170+ sites and apps; published 2024-09-12, updated 2026-04-29 · read on 2026-11-16
- https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-ecommerce-product-page-ux · Baymard Institute, "Product Page UX 2026" · a usability benchmark, 30,000+ scores over 155+ sites; published 2023-10-24, updated 2026-03-18 · read on 2026-11-16
- https://baymard.com/blog/mobile-ux-ecommerce · Baymard Institute, "Mobile UX Trends 2026" · a usability benchmark, 71,000+ rated elements over 150+ mobile sites; published 2024-05-23, updated 2026-07-14 · read on 2026-11-16
- https://baymard.com/blog/homepage-carousel · Baymard Institute, "10 UX Requirements for a User-Friendly Homepage Carousel Design" · usability testing; published 2019-04-30, updated 2025-04-03 · read on 2026-11-16
- https://baymard.com/blog/perceived-security-of-payment-form · Baymard Institute, "How Users Perceive Security During the Checkout Flow" · surveys of perceived security, not of sales · read on 2026-11-16
- https://www.nngroup.com/articles/auto-forwarding/ · Nielsen Norman Group, "Auto-Forwarding Carousels and Accordions Annoy Users and Reduce Visibility" (Jakob Nielsen, 2013-01-19) · one usability test session · read on 2026-11-16

## Experiments
