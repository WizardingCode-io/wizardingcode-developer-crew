# Sources of the SaaS marketing and pricing site playbook

One line per address the playbook cites: the address, its title, what it is, and the date the research for the frontend team's definition read the page itself (`docs/specs/2026-11-16-frontend-team-design.md`, section 1). A source known only from a search summary is not here and no rule cites it. The pages were read through a fetch that returns a model's extraction of the page, and the two papers as PDFs: a figure was seen in the page's text and was not copied by a person. The research's notes stay on the machine that made them. The addresses are provenance: nothing in this file is a step for a session to take.

## The addresses, in the order the playbook cites them

- https://www.exp-platform.com/Documents/2014%20experimentersRulesOfThumb.pdf · Kohavi, Deng, Longbotham and Xu, "Seven Rules of Thumb for Web Site Experimenters", KDD 2014 · a peer-reviewed industry paper of controlled experiments at Microsoft and Bing, read as a PDF · read on 2026-11-16
- https://stripe.com/guides/state-of-european-checkouts-2022 · Stripe with Edgar, Dunn & Company, "The state of European checkouts in 2022" · a vendor's benchmark of sites against its own criteria, with a survey · read on 2026-11-16
- https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/ · Unbounce, "Conversion Benchmark Report", 2024 edition · a vendor's observational benchmark, with no control group · read on 2026-11-16
