# DevOps

## When

Before a change to a pipeline, to a deployment, to an environment file or to infrastructure code, and before such a change is judged. Find the subject below and read the skill that holds it. The team never triggers a deploy pipeline, never applies or destroys, and deploys nothing to production: each of the six skills holds its own limit in its file's words.

## What the skills hold

- No rule of the six comes from a page: the research for the definition read each page's title and not the page.
- The addresses in sources.md are provenance: they are never opened by the session.
- A session that needs more than the crew's rule says so in its report and leaves the reading to the item that reads the pages.
