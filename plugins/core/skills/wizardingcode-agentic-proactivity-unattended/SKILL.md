# Proactivity and unattended work

## When

Before a trigger, a scheduled or background job or an unattended session is added to a product or changed, and before such a change is judged. What sends, buys, deletes or publishes goes through a person's approval. The two rows that quote the journey's skill are rules of the crew's own unattended session, given as the form the team knows, and no rule of a client's product. The entry is `wizardingcode-agentic`.
