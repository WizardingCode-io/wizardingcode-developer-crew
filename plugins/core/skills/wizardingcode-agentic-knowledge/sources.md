# Sources of Knowledge

Each section is a subject of the team's definition (`docs/specs/2026-11-16-agentic-os-team-design.md`, section 3) with its sources as the definition's row holds them, one a line. The research read the content of some pages and only the answer of others; a line that says a page was not opened names nothing that was read. A line is where a subject is read; where a line states a rule of the team's own, a session follows it only as a row of a skill gives it, and never from this file. The addresses are provenance: they are never opened by the session, and nothing in this file is a step for a session to take.

## Knowledge

- Retrieval over the client's documents and data. Anthropic's "Introducing Contextual Retrieval", of 2024-09-19, figures as Anthropic's own: "Reranked Contextual Embedding and Contextual BM25 reduced the top-20-chunk retrieval failure rate by 67% (5.7% → 1.9%).", and under 200,000 tokens of knowledge "you can just include the entire knowledge base in the prompt that you give the model, with no need for RAG or similar methods." (https://www.anthropic.com/research/contextual-retrieval)
- "Citations" (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/citations) and "Prompt caching", of which the research read the first 100,000 characters (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-caching)
- Chroma's studies, "Evaluating Chunking Strategies for Retrieval" (https://www.trychroma.com/research/evaluating-chunking) and "Context Rot: How Increasing Input Tokens Impacts LLM Performance" (https://www.trychroma.com/research/context-rot)
- the papers on agentic retrieval and GraphRAG: "Is Agentic RAG worth it? An experimental comparison of RAG approaches" (https://arxiv.org/html/2601.07711v1), "RAG vs. GraphRAG: A Systematic Evaluation and Key Insights" (https://arxiv.org/html/2502.11371) and "Do We Still Need GraphRAG? Benchmarking RAG and GraphRAG for Agentic Search Systems", at its abstract alone (https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09666)
- MTEB, at its repository (https://github.com/embeddings-benchmark/mteb), its live leaderboard not opened and with no address here, and "MTEB-BR: A Text Embedding Benchmark for Brazilian Portuguese", at its abstract, no benchmark for European Portuguese being found (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04581)
- pgvector, under the PostgreSQL Licence (https://github.com/pgvector/pgvector), and the engines of the research's table, each read as a repository record on 2026-11-16: pgvectorscale, PostgreSQL Licence (https://github.com/timescale/pgvectorscale), ParadeDB, AGPL-3.0 in its community edition, its enterprise edition commercial (https://github.com/paradedb/paradedb), sqlite-vec, Apache-2.0, before its version 1 (https://github.com/asg017/sqlite-vec), libSQL, MIT (https://github.com/tursodatabase/libsql), MariaDB, GPL-2.0 (https://github.com/MariaDB/server), Elasticsearch, under AGPL-3.0, SSPL-1 or the Elastic Licence 2.0 (https://github.com/elastic/elasticsearch), OpenSearch, Apache-2.0 (https://github.com/opensearch-project/OpenSearch), Typesense, GPL-3.0 (https://github.com/typesense/typesense), Meilisearch, MIT with enterprise parts under BUSL-1.1 (https://github.com/meilisearch/meilisearch), Qdrant, Apache-2.0 (https://github.com/qdrant/qdrant), Weaviate, BSD-3-Clause outside its `wl` directory (https://github.com/weaviate/weaviate), Milvus, Apache-2.0 (https://github.com/milvus-io/milvus), Chroma, Apache-2.0 (https://github.com/chroma-core/chroma), LanceDB, Apache-2.0 (https://github.com/lancedb/lancedb), Redis, under RSALv2, SSPLv1 or AGPLv3 since Redis 8 (https://github.com/redis/redis), Vespa, Apache-2.0 (https://github.com/vespa-engine/vespa), Pinecone, a proprietary hosted service, read at its server's README (https://github.com/pinecone-io/pinecone-mcp), and turbopuffer, a proprietary hosted service (https://turbopuffer.com/docs)
- `laravel/ai`, "AI SDK", in Laravel's 13.x documentation (https://laravel.com/docs/13.x/ai-sdk), and the Vercel AI SDK's "RAG Agent guide" (https://ai-sdk.dev/cookbook/guides/rag-chatbot)
