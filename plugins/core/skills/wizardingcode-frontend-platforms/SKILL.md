# Platforms

## When

After `wizardingcode-frontend-strategy`, before the first screen of an application on iOS, Android, macOS or Windows, native or in a cross-platform framework or a web-technology shell, and again before a change to its navigation, its window, its targets or its text sizes. A page on the web takes `wizardingcode-frontend-screens` and not this skill.

The mobile and the desktop phases are not yet proven on a real project. The definition builds the team web first, then mobile, then desktop, each phase proven on a real project (`docs/specs/2026-11-16-frontend-team-design.md`, section 1): until its phase is proven, a choice made on these rows is recorded with the row's label, and the project the change belongs to is that phase's first proof, not a second one.

Signing keys and store credentials never reach the model, and a build, a store submission or an update is a person's decision, by the definition's row "Mobile and desktop toolchains" (section 4): the session prepares the change and says what the person has to do, and the person does it. Signing needs credentials an agent cannot create, so an item that ends in a distributed build names who holds them, for a desktop build and for a phone build sent to a store alike.

## How to read a row

Each rule is a row: the rule, its label, its source and what the source is. The label is the one a design choice built on the rule is recorded with, unless this site measured it.

- `norm`: a standard or a law requires it, named by its clause or its article. No row of this skill is a norm: every size on a phone or a desktop is a platform's guidance.
- `platform-guidance`: the platform's owner asks for it, on the platform the rule names, or a framework's own documentation says how to follow its platforms. The rule begins with the platform it holds on.
- `published-study`: one study, benchmark or test found it; the last cell says what is known of its replication.
- `hypothesis`: a practitioner's convention, a vendor's figure, a release note's stated fact, the research's own inference or the crew's own rule. It is a reasoned default, and no study stands behind it.

The addresses are provenance: they are recorded with a choice and never opened by the session.

The gate's reference (`plugins/core/skills/crew/references/gate.md`) and the frontend standard (`plugins/core/skills/crew/references/frontend.md`) hold no rule of a native platform yet: on a phone and on a desktop these rows are the team's rules until the phases are built.

## Where the sources disagree

The project's `DESIGN.md` wins among conventions and defaults, never below a rule labelled `norm`, never over a gate's result, and with a value looser than the frontend standard's only with the user's decision on record.

- **Touch target across platforms**: 44 pt on iOS, 48 dp on Android, 40 px on Windows, 28 pt by default on macOS with larger tap regions under touch, 1 cm by a practitioner's guidance, and on the web 24 CSS px by the norm (2.5.8). Every value but the web's is a platform's guidance and none binds another platform. Default: each platform's own value on its own platform, so a component shared across platforms takes its hit size from the platform it is drawn on and never one size for all; on the web the default of `wizardingcode-frontend-screens` holds.
- **Text size**: 17 pt by default on iOS, 13 pt on macOS, at least 12 px regular on Windows, and text in `sp` scaled up to 200% on Android. Default: the platform's own text styles at their default size, and every screen checked at the platform's largest text size.
- **Reach on a phone**: Apple calls the middle and the bottom of the screen easier to reach, and the observational study behind thumb zones finds grips mixed, changing every few seconds and drawn to the centre. Default: the primary action in the middle or the bottom of the screen, and no fixed map of reach.
- **Window chrome**: the shells let an app draw its own title bar, and the platforms' guidance asks for the system's states, menus and snap layouts. Default: the system's title bar; a custom one only where the direction asks for it, and then it gives back what the system gave, dragging, the window controls, the snap layouts and the inactive state.

## What the team never claims

- No size on a phone or a desktop is a norm: each is a platform's guidance on its own platform.
- One component carries no single hit size across iOS, Android and Windows.
- The observational study of grips justifies no fixed map of reach, and it predates today's larger phones.
- A store's approval is not proof of accessibility or of quality.
- A framework's figure of version is the fact of the day it was read.
- The mobile and the desktop phases are not proven until a real project proves them.

## Done when

Every choice the change made on a layout, a target, a text size, a material, a navigation container, a window or a store's rule is recorded with the label its row gives, as `wizardingcode-frontend-strategy` says, the change holds on each platform it ships to by that platform's own rows, no step of it needs a signing key or a store credential from the model, and the gates pass.
