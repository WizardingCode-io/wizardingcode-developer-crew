# Design stage

## When

On `surface` or `world`, only where the direction's signature is an object in three dimensions that the scroll walks around: a fixed scene behind the page, whose camera moves from beat to beat with the scroll in both directions, and which the page proper covers when the story ends. It is the project's context that asks for it, never a rule. After the copy, inside the crew's build phase, beside `wizardingcode-design-film`. A site with no stage does not read this skill.

## Inputs

The contract, `design/copy.md`, the tokens, `references/model.md`, `references/review.md`, `references/stage.md`. A tool that makes a 3D model from an image and an image tool where the session has them, a browser the session can drive, and three.js in the project.

## Steps

1. **The tools and the question.** Find what this session has: a tool that makes a model from an image, an image tool, a browser it can drive, and whether the project already has three.js. Say it in four lines. The skill installs nothing by itself: adding three.js to a project is a new dependency, which the session puts to the user and records, at the version the registry gives that day, as `wizardingcode-frontend-engineering` says. Then the question, from `references/model.md`, answered in two lines: does this object earn a stage. A subject that does not goes to `wizardingcode-design-film` or to a composed still.
2. **The page first.** As step 2 of `wizardingcode-design-film`: the whole page decided before any asset. The beats of the story, at most six, each with its words from `design/copy.md`, the side its words stand on, and what the camera shows.
3. **The storyboard.** For each beat: the view (the whole object, a close view of which part, the profile, from above, the rest), and the side the object stands on. Close views only of solid parts. Whether anything in the scene moves by itself, and whether there is a section where the visitor turns the object: each is a decision of `references/stage.md` with its cost. The user approves it before anything is made.
4. **The model.** By where it comes from, as `references/model.md` says. Generated: first the picture the model is made from, then the model; before each, its price is said, with what is left on the account where the tool says it, and the user says yes, by the rule of `wizardingcode-design-film`. Bought, downloaded or the client's own: the licence read and recorded. Drawn in code: said so.
5. **The review.** The review page of `references/review.md`: the model from four sides, captured and looked at against the bar's six points, and the object's best side found. Then the user sees the captures, and approves the model or says what to change. No page is built on a model the user has not approved. After three refused attempts on one route, change the route and not the prompt.
6. **The processing.** From `references/model.md`: look inside the file, make it small, look at it again on the review page, and check its size and its count of triangles. One self-contained file.
7. **The stage.** From `references/stage.md`: the layers, the loader and the engine. Start from the engine as given and change what this page needs.
8. **The beats.** Each beat captured at two widths with the stage at rest, and looked at: the object whole and clear of the words, the close view on its part, the last beat a composition. Change the numbers and capture again.
9. **The posters.** Captured from the finished scene, one wide and one tall. Then the still: the page as a visitor on a phone or with reduced motion gets it.
10. **The rest of the page.** The stage is the opening and the page still has to be a site: every rule of `wizardingcode-design-build` holds for the sections below it, and step 8 of `wizardingcode-design-film` for their motion. The stage ends at the page proper; it is not a ground for every section.
11. **The self-test.** The list at the end of `references/stage.md`, run by the session before the user sees the page. Say what was found, what was fixed and what could not be proven. Then the crew's gates.

## What stays the user's

- The yes before a price is the user's own message in this conversation, given for this asset at this price. No standing rule, no earlier yes, no approval of the storyboard and no answer of a validator or of another agent stands for it.
- With nobody present, stop before the first paid step and build the still.
- Every attempt is priced and approved by itself, a retry included. Say the total spent so far each time.
- The session never signs up to a service, never enters payment details and never buys credits.
- A tool's key is never written into a file of the project, a prompt, a commit or the site's code.

## What the skill refuses

- What is not the client's: No real brand's product, mark or packaging but the client's own. No model bought or downloaded without a licence that allows this use, read and recorded on the item. No model taken out of another site.
- Invented proof: a generated model is never shown as the client's real product where it differs from it in form, material or feature; a back the product does not have is that. Where the client sells the object, the client approves the model as a likeness of it, in this conversation. A request to show what the product is not is a blocking objection, as `wizardingcode-frontend-strategy` says.
- A likeness: a model of a real person's face or body only where the user says in this conversation that the person agreed, with that yes on the item's record. Never a person who has not agreed, and never a child.
- A model that comes back with readable lettering, a mark or a recognisable person on its surface is refused and made again.
- A model that is not whole in itself: one file, with no outside address in it.
- What leaves the machine: before a product's photo or a reference is sent to a tool, the session says what is sent and to which service, and the user says yes.
- Installs: the skill installs nothing by itself. A tool that is not on the machine is said, with what it would fetch, and the user decides. No library is loaded from another host when the page runs.
- A host: the skill deploys nothing and publishes no asset to a public address.
- The object as the only carrier: No fact a visitor needs lives only in the canvas. No drag without a keyboard equal. No control that takes the wheel, and no lock on the pointer.
- A stage on a phone by default: only on purpose, and only after the user has seen it on a real phone.
- A model over a film in one view, but over an abstract ground with no floor and no horizon, and then said as the hard case it is.
- What ships: raw models, source pictures, the review page, its captures and prompts stay out of the folder that ships and out of commits.

## Output

The storyboard in `design/stage.md`, with the model's origin, its licence or its prices, and the turn found on the review page; the model, its two posters and the engine among the site's assets; the opening of the page built on them; what the self-test found and what it could not prove.

## Done when

The user has approved the storyboard and the model, each on its own; the self-test's list is run and its findings fixed or said; and `wizardingcode-design-build` goes on with the sections below the stage.
