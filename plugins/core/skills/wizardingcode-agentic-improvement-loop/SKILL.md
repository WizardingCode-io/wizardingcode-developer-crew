# Improvement loop

## When

Before traces, an eval suite or anything the system learns is added to a product or changed, and before such a change is judged. What the system learns enters use only with provenance, version and a person's approval, and no agent rewrites its own instructions without them. The crew's files hold no rule of their own on how error analysis is done. The entry is `wizardingcode-agentic`.

## What this skill does not hold

- No rule here comes from a page: the research for the definition read the content of some pages and only the answer of others.
- The addresses in sources.md are provenance: they are never opened by the session.
- A session that needs more than the crew's rule says so in its report and leaves the reading to the item that reads the pages.
