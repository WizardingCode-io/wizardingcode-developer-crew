# Selection

## When

Before a library, a tool or a provider is chosen among candidates, and before such a choice is judged. A price or a feature the choice rests on is read at its primary source and holds for three months. The entry is `wizardingcode-research`.

## Rules written from the sources

Each row is a rule the crew wrote in its own words from one of the sources the user gave on 2026-11-15, named in the second cell. No gate of the crew rules by a row of this table and a reviewer names none as a defect: where a row disagrees with a row of the crew's rules, the crew's rule holds. No sentence of a source is copied, no source is installed by this skill, and a session opens none.

| Rule | Source |
| :- | :- |
| Whatever the choice between the two leading candidates turns on, a cell of the comparison, a price or a measured figure, is confirmed by a second source. | `bmad` |
| The scores behind a verdict are shown, so that a reader who weighs the requirements differently can reach a verdict of their own. | `bmad` |
| What a winner must do is settled with those who decide, not found by searching, and before any candidate is looked at. | `bmad` |
| A vendor's account of its own product is set beside what independent users report, and where the two differ the report shows both. | `bmad` |

- A session that needs more than the crew's rule says so in its report and leaves the reading to the item that reads the pages.
