# Sources of Routing and measurement

Each section is a subject of the team's definition (`docs/specs/2026-11-16-agentic-os-team-design.md`, section 3) with its sources as the definition's row holds them, one a line. The research read the content of some pages and only the answer of others; a line that says a page was not opened names nothing that was read. A line is where a subject is read; where a line states a rule of the team's own, a session follows it only as a row of a skill gives it, and never from this file. The addresses are provenance: they are never opened by the session, and nothing in this file is a step for a session to take.

## Evaluation

- Of the right decision, repeated, and of the task. What is measured, as decided: per project, a set of the client's real cases and a closed set of tools and flows
- the assistant chooses the right tool or flow in more than 90% of the cases
- each case is run several times and counted right only if right every time
- the task's final outcome is measured apart. Anthropic's "Demystifying evals for AI agents", of 2026-01-09 (https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents), and the Claude platform's page on evals, at the address the QA team's definition opened (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/test-and-evaluate/develop-tests), of which the research read the first 100,000 characters
- Hamel Husain and Shreya Shankar's "AI Evals: Everything You Need to Know" (https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/), and "Who Validates the Validators? Aligning LLM-Assisted Evaluation of LLM Outputs with Human Preferences", by Shankar and others, of 2024-04-18 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12272)
- Eugene Yan's "An LLM-as-Judge Won't Save The Product—Fixing Your Process Will", of 2025-04-20 (https://eugeneyan.com/writing/eval-process/)
- tau-bench, with its limits: the paper that introduces pass^k, "τ-bench: A Benchmark for Tool-Agent-User Interaction in Real-World Domains", of 2024-06-17 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12045), and Sierra's board (https://taubench.com/), whose entries carry no date and which shows one run alone (pass^1) without defining it, the results over repeated runs not being found on it
