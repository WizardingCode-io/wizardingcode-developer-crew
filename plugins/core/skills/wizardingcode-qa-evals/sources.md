# Sources of Evals

Each section is a subject of the team's definition (`docs/specs/2026-11-15-qa-team-design.md`, section 3) with its sources as the definition's row holds them, one a line. The research opened each page and read its title, not the page end to end; a line that says a page was not opened, or that names a paid book, names nothing that was read. A line is where a subject is read and no line is a rule. The addresses are provenance: they are never opened by the session, and nothing in this file is a step for a session to take.

## Testing systems with AI

- The crew's own eval suite (`plugins/core/evals`, `docs/evals`) and the documentation of `claude plugin eval`, "Test plugins with evals" (https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugin-evals)
- Anthropic: the documentation on evals, one page titled "Define success criteria and build evaluations" (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/test-and-evaluate/develop-tests), and the engineering article on evals of agents, "Demystifying evals for AI agents", of 2026-01-09 (https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents)
- Hamel Husain's "Your AI Product Needs Evals" (https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals/) and his guide to an LLM as a judge, "Using LLM-as-a-Judge For Evaluation: A Complete Guide" (https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/llm-judge/), and Eugene Yan on evals, "Evaluating the Effectiveness of LLM-Evaluators (aka LLM-as-Judge)" (https://eugeneyan.com/writing/llm-evaluators/)
- the documentation of promptfoo (https://www.promptfoo.dev/docs/intro/), of Inspect, of the UK AISI, named today the UK AI Security Institute (https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/) and of DeepEval (https://deepeval.com/docs/getting-started)
- the evals documentation of the model provider the project uses: OpenAI's "Working with evals" (https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evals), Google's "Gen AI evaluation service overview" (https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/evaluation-overview)
- TypeSafe's documentation, for typed judgements (https://docs.typesafe.ai/)
- the architects' definition for AI and agents (`docs/specs/2026-11-15-architects-team-design.md`, section 3, the row "AI and agents")
