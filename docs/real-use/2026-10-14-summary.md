# Real use of SP1: the closing summary

- Phase: 2026-10-12 to 2026-10-14 (planned to 2026-10-26; closed early by the user's decision of 2026-10-14, "terminar o plugin e passar para prod").
- Plugin under use at the close: 0.1.7, installed at user scope from the marketplace.
- Records: [the plan](README.md), [findings](findings.md) (1 to 20), [corpus](corpus.md) (C-1 to C-17).

## What happened

The user took the plugin into real work on the day it was released and kept it there: five projects, not the three of the plan, 24 work items in three days, all in auto mode, two or three sessions at a time on one checkout. Every day of use produced findings, and every day of findings produced a release: 0.1.0 to 0.1.4 on 2026-10-12, 0.1.5 and 0.1.6 on 2026-10-14, each for one or two findings. On 2026-10-14 the user called that loop what it was, a loop, and the phase changed shape: findings are recorded as they come and fixed in one release at the close, the records are brought up to date in batch from the projects' `.crew/work/` folders, and the SP2 design is drafted in parallel. 0.1.7 is that release.

Twenty findings in three days; thirteen fixed outright, three partly, four carried forward with a named owner. The two that cost most, #8 (eleven items said Proven while the PHP gates never ran) and #19 (the agent asked the user to type `! crew approve`), were both consequences of a design decision made on a wrong observation (#3) and of a detector that saw one target where there were two; both were visible only in real use.

## What the phase changed in the product

0.1.2 to 0.1.7, all from findings: user-only decisions recorded honestly with the mode they were taken in; the Bash rules judging one simple command at a time; the wait for the user as a permit for one turn; `criteria remove`; one target per manifest, keyed by path and stack; the deps gate on dependency changes only (ADR 0002); the watch list migrating by itself; several lockfiles handled; the user-only verb anchored at the command position; auto mode attended, headless sessions not; `gate run` saying no with its exit code; `start --preserve` and unknown flags refused; the shared checkout named and recorded; `riskPathExclusions` (ADR 0003); delivery from the gate phase.

## What carries into SP2

The design is drafted in [`docs/specs/2026-10-14-sp2-conclave-and-judgement-gates-design.md`](../specs/2026-10-14-sp2-conclave-and-judgement-gates-design.md). From this phase it takes:

- **The corpus as the first eval cases**: C-11 (erasure or deactivation), C-13 (the redirect target), C-16 (the schema came back, the data did not), C-9 (a default is not a consent), and the method cases C-4, C-10 (criterion kinds). Section 15 of the spec lists them as draft cases; they are written in `plugins/core/evals/` in SP2's plan.
- **The validator requirement** (#17): about thirty user-only decisions in three days were taken with no prompt; 0.1.6 made auto mode attended, and the unattended modes still need a role that decides under rules and is named in the record.
- **An `observed` criterion kind** (#15, C-10).
- **The calibration of the debate**: on the seventeen entries, the debate would have opened on three (C-5, C-9, C-11); the gap round alone would have caught C-13 and C-16.

And it leaves to the engine, by name, so they are not relabelled SP2: the proof scope by item (#9's root, #20), the cost of a release (#11, #18) and the risk-path defaults (#1, #14 beyond the exclusions).

## What was not measured

- The fast route outside this repository, the ai-mem first use without `.crew/`, and the interactive paths (the accepted skip, the `PermissionRequest` path, the mod's panel) in a session with a person present. Real use never produced a fast item in a client project; the user's work is bigger than that.
- The full Pest suite and Pint under the engine on a Laravel item: the next gate run of C-15 does it, and whatever it shows is a finding like any other, recorded against 0.1.7.
- The user-only decisions under 0.1.6 and 0.1.7, where auto mode shows the prompt: every session with crew activity so far started before the 0.1.6 restart.

These are the first things SP2's own real use will see, in the same five projects.
