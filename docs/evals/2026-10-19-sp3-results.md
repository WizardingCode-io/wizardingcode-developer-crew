## What the runs showed

**The skill page is what the fast route knows.** Task 3 put `--source`, the triage and the questions' document channel in `references/receive.md` only, to keep the page under its ceiling; the first runs of both reception cases never recorded a source. One Receive row and one parenthesis on the page (five sentences shortened elsewhere, a skill test pinning them) made the ticket case pass in full and the document case post its question beside the document. The page is the product on the fast route; a reference the page does not name does not exist there (finding 92).

**A verdict only tightens, and the engine holds it.** With the canned answers in their right shape, the validator approval was refused at 0.97 in both runs that asked; no path around it was found by the engine's rules, and the third run's agent said so in the words rule 15 uses. The second run's agent rewrote its own conclave and retried, which the fixture's dispute survived but the real Jev might not: the dispute should be sticky for the item (finding 94).

**A fixture's word is only as good as its shape.** The jev case's first run cost 1.88 USD because an answer without `type` made Jev unavailable and decision 12 let the approval proceed unverified; the README's paragraph now says so beside the format. The canned path is read from the folder `crew` runs in, since the kit may move the scaffold.

**An issue is analysed before it is fixed.** `issue-root-cause-named` passed first time: the earlier item the fixture built with the installed `crew` (a chore proven on the fast route, two roles from the SubagentStop hook, a task note) was named with its commit by the analysis, and the ending named it back.

**The sandbox denies the plugin's own files at times.** The third document run could not read `references/conclave.md`; the first two could (finding 95). Until the kit's permission model for reads outside the scaffold is read, a run whose trace shows the denial is re-run, not scored.

**Cost.** 3.49 USD for eleven runs. The cases that stop before build cost 0.11 to 0.31 USD; the issue's fast item 0.15; the one run that went through the full route 1.88. The plan estimated 0.2 USD per case; the reception and jev cases are within it once their defects were out, as the design intends: the behaviours they measure happen before any build.
