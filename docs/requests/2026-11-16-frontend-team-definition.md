# Frontend team: subjects, sources, tools, MCP servers and skills

## Context

After the definitions of the backend developer team (`docs/specs/2026-11-14-backend-team-stacks-design.md`), the security team (`docs/specs/2026-11-15-security-team-design.md`), the ETL team (`docs/specs/2026-11-15-etl-team-design.md`), the researchers team (`docs/specs/2026-11-15-researchers-team-design.md`), the architects team (`docs/specs/2026-11-15-architects-team-design.md`), the QA team (`docs/specs/2026-11-15-qa-team-design.md`), the DevOps team (`docs/specs/2026-11-15-devops-team-design.md`) and the agentic-os team (`docs/specs/2026-11-16-agentic-os-team-design.md`), the frontend team is the last team to define. The engine has the team (`packages/engine/src/team.ts`, `TEAMS.frontend`), three packs (`packs/frontend`, `packs/nuxt`, `packs/next`), two rubrics and six design skills, and nothing says where the team reads about what it builds, which tools it runs, or which MCP servers and skills it uses.

The user chose a written definition first, decided with them point by point on 2026-11-16. Twenty subjects were confirmed first. Asked for the sources, the user chose to give context first ("Dou contexto primeiro"), gave it, and asked for a deep research of everything, skills included. The research was done the same day in two rounds (`reports/Equipa frontend pesquisa profunda.md` and thirteen notes under `research_notes/Equipa frontend pesquisa profunda/`, whose register of primary sources and whose corrections win over the first round's notes). After the report the user decided the scope, the promise, the kinds of project and the proof of the new team, confirmed twenty-eight subjects in place of the twenty, and took the complete base of sources. Two readings of the same day then read the most-starred design and frontend skills on GitHub and the tools' own sets, and the MCP servers, from their own files; they win over the notes wherever they differ.

## The definition (decided 2026-11-16)

### What the team is

The user's context of 2026-11-16, in their words: "entao eu pensava que ja tinhamos esta euqipa consolidada tivemos semnas de volta disto mas pelos visto nao temos. e pelos resultados vejo que nao. eu quero todas as skills de design e frontend developer noa so a questao de organização de codigo conhecimneto de frameworks mas tambem aquilo que sao as boas praticas de frontend seja web desktop mobile, comunicação de design isso temos muita informação temos tabem utilizar todas as skills de motion, transiçoes, animacoes porporçoes que medidas e padddings espaços conjugaçoa de cores. pixel perfec retina tudo o que uma app de alto nivel ou site de alto nivel precisa esta equipa nao pode em momento algum desenvolver ou aprovar algo que seja de ai slop nao é isso isto tem que ser bem mais rigoroso mais criativo mas baseado na neurociencia em factos numero aquilo que converte mais aquilo que viraliza mais aquilo que vicia mais o user. portatno de uma vez por todas quero uma epsquisa de tudo desde skills, quero as tecnicas os conhecimento des melhor do mundo sekap que for quero que no final quando pedir algo de frotnend nao me seja dado algo default quero algo diferenciado sempre com provas com justificaçoes para cada contexto e cenario nao quero um site para vender um pdf com as mesmas tecncicas para um ecomerce ou site institucional."

The session's reading of it: the user is not satisfied with the frontend results the crew gives today, after weeks spent on it. The team must cover web, desktop and mobile; design craft with numbers (proportions, spacing, colour, type, retina, motion); never build or approve AI slop; every design choice differentiated per context (a page that sells a PDF is neither an e-commerce nor an institutional site) and justified with evidence. A deep research of everything first, skills included.

After the report, four decisions of 2026-11-16, each the option recommended:

- Scope: web, mobile and desktop, by phases. The definition covers the three (subjects, sources, the platforms' and the stores' rules); the building goes web first with the playbooks per kind of project, then mobile (native, Expo, Flutter), then desktop; each phase is proven on a real project. This reverses the earlier refusals of native references (the sources ledger's rows 81, 123 and 142; SP9's scope).
- The promise, "proof with a label": every design decision carries its label (norm, platform guidance, published study with its replication note, measured on this site, hypothesis). A label never upgrades without a measurement, and a vendor's benchmark or case study gets no label of its own: it is said as the vendor's figure and carries the label "hypothesis" until measured on this site. These two points are the session's reading from the research, told to the user on 2026-11-16 with the points taken under the standing rules, and are not the user's words. The team never invokes dopamine, neuroscience or addiction; it uses what works and is lawful (social proof, scarcity and urgency only when true, defaults that serve the user) and never builds a deceptive pattern.
- Kinds of project, ten, each with its playbook, its metric and its evidence, declared with the direction before the first file: 1 info-product sales page; 2 e-commerce; 3 SaaS marketing and pricing site; 4 product UI (web apps, dashboards); 5 institutional and public sector; 6 donations and non-profit; 7 brand, portfolio, agency and campaign; 8 content and media; 9 mobile app; 10 B2B lead generation. A project that fits none declares a new kind with its metric.
- The proof of the new team: after the definition and the building, a run on a real project of the user's, of one of the kinds, with real photographs, judged blind against today's crew by the user and other people the user names, besides the gates. Only then is the team called consolidated. The run closes the one finding 210 still owes.
