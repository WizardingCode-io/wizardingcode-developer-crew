# SP8: New projects: the strategy stage and the audit. Implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task in this session, through the crew method (each task is a crew work item in this repository, as SP7a, SP7b and SP3 were). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.
>
> **Written on 2026-10-19**, from the SP7 design (`docs/specs/2026-10-17-sp7-teams-agents-workflows-and-rules-design.md`, §6.2 (the CTO as the strategy's reviewer on full), §7.1 (the phases of `new-project` and `audit`), §7.3 (the strategy), §7.5 (the audit), §13 (the strategy without a knowledge base, the report in three formats), §15 (the three cases), §16 (the acceptance), §18 decision 4), the rules 3 and 19 of §5, ADR 0007 decision 2 and ADR 0008: SP8 is built right after 0.8.0, with no real-use phase between, and ships as 0.9.0. It carries the debt SP3 deferred (findings 82 to 96) in its first task, and the overhead decision finding 93 left to this plan.

**Goal:** A new project, or a feature the conclave raises to one, gets a strategy before its spec (viability, cost, time, stack, scaling phases), every claim pointing at a researched fact, proportionate to the real need, ruled by the judge and reviewed by the CTO on the full route; an existing codebase gets an end-to-end audit from its own tools run over the whole tree and the reviewers reading the code, delivered as a report under the brand in Markdown, HTML and PDF, every finding carrying a file and a line or a log, signed by a person before it leaves.

**Architecture:** No model runs inside the engine. The two new phases are data in `phasesFor(work)`: `strategy` between `understand` and `specify` for the `new-project` kind, `audit` for the `audit` kind, which is always full and has no build or gate phase. The strategy is a document the engine checks for structure and sources (`strategy.ts`): each claim ends with a mark naming a fact line of `conclave/researcher.md`; the judgement gate `strategy` is ruled by the judge from the CTO's findings on full, from the files alone on standard, and recorded through `crew judgement record` as every ruling is. The audit is two commands: `crew audit <id> run` runs the project's own tools over the whole tree with no baseline through the gate runners and lists the files the reviewers read (`audit-run.ts`); `crew audit <id> report` parses the findings files the roles wrote, drops what carries no proof, and renders the report in three formats (`audit-report.ts`, `brand.ts`): Markdown and HTML by the engine, PDF printed by the project's own Playwright or by a Chrome binary on the machine, or said as not produced. A person signs with `crew approve <id> audit`; nothing signs an audit unattended.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript (dependency-free engine, Bun for tests, Biome), Node 20+ at run time, the mods API of `plugins/core/hooks/register.js`, `claude plugin eval` for the cases, the project's own Playwright or a Chrome binary for the PDF (`--headless=new --print-to-pdf`), the brand's SVGs (`docs/brand/`, copied into `plugins/core/brand/` with ADR 0001's licence note).

**Spec:** the SP7 design, sections named above. The rules the engine enforces in this release: 3 (a remote test environment like production is always recommended: the strategy reference and the audit report say it when absent), 19 (deliverables under our brand: the report renderer), and the two rules of discernment of §7.3 as the strategy rubric's questions.

**Decisions taken while writing this plan** (each a reading of the spec where it left a detail open; the leads may return any):

1. **The kinds and the phases.** `WorkType` gains `audit` and so does the `work_type` choice; `WorkKind` gains `audit`, derived from `work_type: audit` alone (an answered `kind: audit` with another `work_type` is refused: "an audit is work_type audit"). `Phase` gains `strategy` (after `understand`, before `specify` in `PHASE_ORDER`) and `audit` (after `recognize`). `phasesFor`: `new-project` is the route's list with `strategy` inserted before `specify` (never fast, as 0.8.0 already floors it); `audit` is `[receive, recognize, audit, deliver]` on every route and the route is raised to `full` at start naming the kind ("kind audit is always full"). An audit item needs no criterion: `crew start` accepts none for it and `crew status` says "audit: the proof is the signed report"; the Stop hook has nothing to hold since an audit changes no watched file.
2. **A feature raised to a strategy is a command, not a reclassification.** `crew kind <id> raise --reason "<why>"` (the only raise is to `new-project`): allowed while the phase is `understand`, `specify` or `conclave`; sets `kind: new-project` and `kindFrom: "raised: <reason>"`, applies the standard floor, and when the phase is `specify` or `conclave` moves it back to `strategy` saying so (the spec and the conclave files stay and are re-read after the strategy, which may change decisions; no approval exists yet, since the spec's approval is the guard into `plan`); from `plan` on it is refused ("a strategy comes before the plan; from plan on, a new item"). The raise is the lead's, recorded in `routeReasons` and appended to the decision register as the agent's.
3. **The strategy document and its guard.** `strategy.md` in the work folder with five sections `## Viability`, `## Cost`, `## Time`, `## Stack`, `## Scaling phases`. The first four hold bullets (`- …`); every bullet ends with one or more marks `[R<n>]`, `n` the 1-based line number of `conclave/researcher.md` whose line starts with `- ` (a fact line); a bullet with no mark, or a mark on a line that is not a fact line, is refused naming the bullet and the line; `[K:<id>]` marks are refused until SP4 ("knowledge entries arrive with SP4"). The fifth section holds a table `| Trigger | Change | Cost |` with at least one body row whose three cells are non-empty (the design: an empty scaling table is refused on new-project; every project has a first trigger at which its design changes). `STRATEGY_SECTIONS` and `checkStrategy(text, researcherText)` live in `strategy.ts`, beside `CONCLAVE_SECTIONS`' style. The guard out of `strategy` (into `specify`): the structure holds, the researcher file exists, and the `strategy` judgement is proven on the current hash of the two files. The engine says, in `crew status` while the phase is `strategy` and in the report's Strategy section, "strategy from the research alone (no knowledge base until SP4)"; the strategy reference tells the lead to write the same line under Stack.
4. **The strategy judgement gate.** `JudgementGateName` gains `strategy`; it is planned for every item whose phases include `strategy` (`plannedStrategyJudgement(work)`, not from the gate run); its reviewer is the CTO on full (`reviewer: "cto"`, the seat "reviewer of the strategy" in `plannedSeats` on full for the new-project kind, §6.2) and nobody on standard (`reviewer: "judge"`: the judge reads the two files against the rubric). `crew strategy <id> check` validates the structure, writes `review/strategy/inputs.json` (`{ strategy: "strategy.md", researcher: "conclave/researcher.md", hash }`, engine-owned) and prints the paths and the hash; the ruling is recorded with `crew judgement <id> record --ruling <file>` (gate `strategy`, stamped with `scopeHash` over the two files' contents; a ruling on changed files is `stale`); the judgement cap (two rounds) and `crew continue <id> judgement` apply as today. `rubrics/strategy.md`: proportion (each element the stack or the design proposes is required by a stated need with a source; over-engineering is named), proof (every claim's mark says what the claim says), cost and time measured (a figure with its basis, never a guess), the scaling table (each trigger observable, each change named, each cost given), persuasion (where the request asked for more, the smaller answer is argued point by point). The CTO agent gains "As reviewer of the strategy" (the reviewers' findings shape: `F-n · defect|advisory · strategy.md:<line> · <sentence>`); the judge agent gains "As judge of the strategy" (the ruling shape it already returns, gate `strategy`).
5. **The research pass comes first on a new project.** The strategy reference launches the researcher before the strategy, with the brief's open questions and the five strategy questions (what exists today for this need, what the documentation of the candidate stacks says, cost and time references, limits, maintenance), saved as `conclave/researcher.md` and verified with `crew research <id> verify` as the conclave reference asks; at the conclave the same researcher is resumed with the open decisions (never relaunched), its answer appended to the same file, and the verification run again on the new hash. The report's Strategy section lists the sections' bullet counts, the scaling rows, the ruling and the CTO's findings count.
6. **The audit run is the gate runners over the whole tree with no baseline.** `crew audit <id> run [--only a,b]`, allowed in phase `audit` alone: `runAudit(paths, work, profile, policy, options)` in `audit-run.ts` runs, for every target of the profile, `build`, `test`, `lint` and `typecheck` with the profile's commands and `gateTimeoutMs`, `deps` through `depsAuditCommand` listing every advisory (`baselined: false`, no base commit), `secrets` over the whole tree with no base; `e2e` is never run (recorded `unavailable: needs the environment; run by hand`). Each run's log is `logs/audit-<gate>-<target>.txt`; the record is `audit/runs.json` `{ at, head, runs: [{ gate, target, status: "pass" | "fail" | "unavailable", durationMs, log, reason?, advisories? }] }` (engine-owned); beside it `audit/files.txt` (every watched source file of the tree, as `review/files.txt` lists a change's) and `audit/scope.json` `{ targets: [{ path, stack, packageManager }], files, bytes, riskAreas }` with the risk areas from `riskAreasForPaths` over every file. The engine writes nothing of the project; the tools run as a gate run runs them. A failing test or build is a `fail` run with its log, never a blocked audit: the audit continues and the report names it.
7. **The audit's seats and findings files.** `crew team <id>` on an audit seats the researcher (dependencies), security, architecture, code quality, test quality, and data or infra when `scope.riskAreas` names `data-migration` or `infra`, each with the reason `audit`; the CTO does not sit. The roles write `audit/<section>.findings.md` for `security`, `architecture`, `code-quality`, `test-quality`, `data`, `infra` in the reviewers' shape (`# <section> findings for <id>`, `## Findings`, `- F-n · defect|advisory · <path>:<line> · <sentence>`, or `· log:<path under logs/> ·` for a finding a run proves); the researcher writes `audit/dependencies.findings.md` as a table `| Package | Current | Latest | Pros | Cons | Risk | Proof |`; the lead (the session) writes `audit/business.findings.md` in the findings shape (a flow a client cannot complete, a rule unenforced, read from the README, the routes, the domain and the records), `audit/summary.md` (one paragraph) and `audit/recommendations.md` as a table `| # | Recommendation | Cost | Time | Findings |` (Cost `S`, `M` or `L`; Time in days; Findings the F-ids). Each reviewer's agent file gains one paragraph: on an audit the prompt names `.crew/work/<id>/audit/files.txt` and no diff; the files listed are the change; `## Outside the change` is `none`. `references/audit.md` carries the flow and the prompts.
8. **The report, its proof check and its formats.** `crew audit <id> report`: `renderAuditReport(inputs)` in `audit-report.ts` parses the findings files (`parseFindingsFile(text)` → `{ id, severity, path, line, log, text }[]`, shared with nothing yet: the judge's rulings carry findings in JSON), checks every finding's proof (a `path:line` whose file exists under the root, is not a symlink out of it, and has at least that many lines; or a `log:` path under the work folder's `logs/` that exists) and leaves out the rest, counted and named in the method section ("left out for want of proof: security F-3 …"). The sections in order: Summary and verdict (the lead's paragraph; the verdict computed: `critical` with any security defect or a secret found, `attention` with any other defect, `sound` with none; the counts per section and severity); Scope and method (targets, files, bytes; what ran with pass or fail; what was only read; what could not run with its reason; the findings left out); Business issues; Security issues (the findings and the secrets scan); Dependencies to update (the researcher's table, plus the `deps` advisories as rows with `Proof: log`); Architecture and code quality (the findings; debt as counts per file); Tests (what ran, what could not, log paths); Recommendations (the lead's rows ordered by cost `S` < `M` < `L`, then time ascending); Appendix (each log's path, size and last twenty lines). The Markdown is `audit/report.md`; the HTML `audit/report.html` is one self-contained file from a template in `audit-report.ts` with the lockup and the mark inlined from `brand.ts` (`brandDir()`: `WIZARDINGCODE_CREW_BRAND_DIR`, then `$CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT/brand`, then `<dirname of the running bundle>/../brand`, then `<root>/docs/brand`; none found: the header says "brand assets not found" and the page renders without them), the project's name, the date, the item id, the signature line ("unsigned" until the approval). The PDF `audit/report.pdf` by `printPdf(html, out, env)`: the project's own `playwright` or `@playwright/test` resolved from the root with `createRequire` (as `e2e/playwright.mjs` resolves it) → `chromium.launch()` → `page.pdf({ format: "A4", printBackground: true })`; else a browser binary: `WIZARDINGCODE_CREW_BROWSER`, then `google-chrome`, `chromium`, `chromium-browser` on PATH, then the macOS app paths of Google Chrome and Chromium, run as `<bin> --headless=new --disable-gpu --no-pdf-header-footer --print-to-pdf=<out> file://<html>` with a 60 s timeout; none, a hang or a non-zero exit: `pdf: null` with `pdfReason` ("PDF not produced: no Playwright in the project and no browser found (set WIZARDINGCODE_CREW_BROWSER)", or the exit and the tail of stderr). `audit/report.json` `{ at, hash, md, html, pdf, pdfReason, leftOut, counts, verdict, signed }` (engine-owned; `hash` over the findings files, `runs.json`, `summary.md` and `recommendations.md`). The command exits 0 when the Markdown and the HTML are written; the PDF's absence is said, never hidden.
9. **A person signs.** `Approval.what` gains `audit`; `crew approve <id> audit` is user-only and `--mode` is refused always ("an audit's report is signed by a person: run crew await <id> user and end the turn"), in the hook's rules and in the CLI; the approval needs `report.json` current against the files' hash, re-renders the three formats with the signature line ("Signed on <date> by the user, relayed by the agent" under `relay`, "by the user" under `prompt`) and appends to the register. ## Global Constraints

- Every task is a crew work item in this repository: `crew start "<request>" --answers '<json>' --criterion "…" --preserve "…"`, the failing test and `crew tdd red` before the code, `crew tdd green`, `crew tdd before`/`after` for a preserve criterion (`before` alone, before any source change; one quoted string after `--`, finding 91), one `crew gate run <id>`, the judgement gates (standard route: code-quality and test-quality; the user's rule: at most two rounds, the second scoped to the first's defects, advisories to `docs/real-use/findings.md`), `crew judgement record`, the leads' validations of the spec (at most two rounds; a lead is resumed with a message for round 2, never relaunched) and of the delivery, commit on `main`, push after each task.
- The `crew` on PATH is the installed plugin, **0.8.0**, for the whole of SP8; the new commands and hooks are exercised by the tests and by `node plugins/core/dist/crew.mjs …` after `bun run build`, never by this repository's own items until 0.9.0 is installed. Where a hook of the working tree would have recorded something the installed one does not, the record says so.
- `bun run check` green before every commit; `bun run format` fixes lint. Tasks that touch `plugins/core` (every task) also run `bun run plugin:check`. `bun run build` after every engine change (the bundle is committed).
- The engine has no runtime dependency; imports carry an explicit `.ts`; engine-owned files are written through `writeProtectedJson`/`writeProtectedText`; the hooks fail open (`{}` on any exception); a child process (a gate, a browser) always has a timeout; the engine writes nothing of the audited project.
- **A policy can only tighten**; a gate command is never changed to make a check pass; a judgement ruling is never edited.
- **The fast route launches no role and its cost does not rise**: no new agent file (the CTO and the judge gain a section each); `SKILL.md` stays at or under 9254 bytes.
- Naming: `wizardingcode-developer-crew:<agent>`; variables `WIZARDINGCODE_CREW_*`; data under the user's crew directory; files in English; no client project name in the repository; no real ticket, URL or person in a fixture; the brand's assets under their licence note wherever they are copied.
- The researcher is resumed, never relaunched, within an item (the user's rule on tokens); every role's answer is saved by the SubagentStop hook, never by hand.
- No version bump before Task 6. One release, 0.9.0, at the close.
